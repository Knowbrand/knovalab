Common Inquiries

Digital Menu FAQ

Clear answers about deployment, compatible TVs, templates, and setup.

Can we design our own dynamic template graphics?

Yes. Knovalab CMS supports custom asset uploads, high-resolution photos, and video formats. We also design bespoke menu templates around your typography and customer hierarchy.

What happens if our restaurant internet connection drops?

Your screens keep running. Our on-prem signage system downloads and caches all files locally; a network connection is only needed when publishing new changes.

How many displays can we link to a single server?

Our self-hosted CMS can manage an unlimited number of screens across a local router network. Bandwidth is the main factor when many screens play UHD media.

Do you supply the physical commercial monitor hardware?

We can consult, source, and install professional high-brightness displays built for long daily operation, or configure Android players to plug into your existing HDMI TVs.
