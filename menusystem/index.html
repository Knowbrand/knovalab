Common Inquiries

Digital Menu FAQ

Clear technical answers about deployment, compatible TVs, templates, and setup.

Can we design our own dynamic template graphics?⌄

Yes. Knovalab CMS supports custom asset uploads, high-resolution photos, and video formats. We also design bespoke menu templates mapped precisely around your menu typography and customer hierarchy.

What happens if our restaurant internet connection drops?⌄

Your screens will continue running without interruption. Our on-prem signage ecosystem downloads and caches all files locally; active network connection is only needed when publishing new changes.

How many displays can we link to a single server?⌄

Our self-hosted CMS can manage an unlimited number of screens across a localized router network. Bandwidth is the main factor when multiple screens play UHD media.

Do you supply the physical commercial monitor hardware?⌄

We can consult, source, and install professional high-brightness displays designed for 18/7 operational cycles, or configure Android players to plug directly into your current HDMI-equipped TVs.
