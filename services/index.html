Common Inquiries

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers about our technical solutions, hardware requirements, and network topologies.

Do your signage networks require active internet connections?⌄
No. Our on-premise systems function on your local network. You only require a network switch or Wi-Fi router to sync files from your local dashboard to displays.
What hardware players are compatible with Knovalab signage?⌄
Any player running Android or Android TV can provide a capable high-output display. Hardware media sticks that plug directly into HDMI ports are also supported.
Are there any monthly licensing or platform fees?⌄
No. We build your hardware, CMS software setup, and installation. You own your system with no mandatory recurring costs.
Can we manage screens across separate geographic properties?⌄
Yes. While optimized for single on-prem local networks, our system can be configured to sync across multiple retail branches using secure private VPN tunnels.
