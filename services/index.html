COMMON INQUIRIES

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers about our technical solutions, hardware requirements, and network topology.

Do your signage networks require active internet connections?+

No. Our on-Prem systems operate entirely on your local network. The only time you require a network switch or Wi-Fi router is to sync files from your local dashboard to displays.

What hardware players are compatible with Knovalab signage?+

Any player running Android or Android TV. We can provide an pre-configured plug-and-play Android hardware media stick that plugs directly into HDMI ports on your existing TVs.

Are there any monthly licensing or platform fees?+

No. You pay once for the hardware, CMS software setup, and installation. You own your system with no mandatory recurring costs.

Can we manage screens over geographic properties?+

Yes. While optimized for single on-prem local networks, our system can be configured to sync across multiple retail branches using secure private VPN tunnels.
