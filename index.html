A Practical Difference

Updating a Screen Shouldn’t Require a USB Trip

Both approaches can put content on a screen. The meaningful difference is how much repeated physical work is required when content changes.

Traditional USB Workflow

  • Edit the content
  • Export the file
  • Copy it to a USB drive
  • Walk to the screen
  • Replace the content
  • Repeat for another screen

Knovalab Local Workflow

  • Open the Windows CMS
  • Choose or change content
  • Select one or multiple screens
  • Publish over the local network

Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What does Knovalab build?

Knovalab builds on-prem digital signage, digital menu experiences, websites, and selected custom digital solutions.

Is Knovalab only a website company?

No. Websites remain an important service alongside locally managed display systems and digital menus.

Is the Digital Signage System cloud-based?

The current product is designed for local, on-prem operation rather than a cloud-centered workflow.

Does signage keep playing when the CMS is closed?

After content is synchronized to the Player, required files are stored locally and playback can continue.

What devices can run the signage Player?

The Player is designed for compatible Android and Android TV devices.

Can one CMS publish to multiple screens?

Yes. The CMS can select one or multiple paired screens for publishing.

What is the difference between Signage and Digital Menus?

Digital signage is the broader display system. Digital menus apply screen-based presentation specifically to menu content.

Can I combine Knovalab solutions?

Yes. Depending on the business, a website, signage system, and menu solution may work independently or together.
