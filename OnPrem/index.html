Why On-Prem?

Built Around the Network You Already Have.

“On-prem” simply means the signage workflow is centered around the business’s own local environment instead of a remote cloud dashboard.

Local Management

The business controls the system from its own Windows CMS.

Local-Network Operation

CMS and Players are designed to communicate inside the local environment.

No Cloud-Centered Workflow

The initial product does not require normal local signage management through a cloud dashboard.

Location-Centered Control

The workflow remains centered around infrastructure at the business location.

Operational Value

A USB Is Cheap.
The Repeated Workflow Isn’t.

For one screen changed once every few months, a USB drive may be perfectly adequate. The practical inconvenience grows with frequent changes, more displays, wider properties, and different content across screens.

Frequent Changes

Repeated copying and physical updates become part of regular staff work.

Multiple Screens

The same manual steps must be repeated from display to display.

Spread-Out Displays

Staff may need to move around the property to complete one update.

Different Content

Each screen can require separate files and careful manual handling.

Consistency

A centralized workflow helps selected screens receive the intended content.

Fewer Manual Steps

The CMS removes the need to carry the update physically to every display.
